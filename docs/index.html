What is Langopia?

💡

Langopia is the first complete SaaS platform designed specifically for language academies. It combines academy management, live video classrooms, AI-powered content creation, and student learning apps into one seamless experience. From scheduling classes to generating personalized reports, Langopia handles everything so teachers can focus on what matters most: teaching.

📈

Invisible Progress: Without proper tracking systems, it's impossible to measure student progress, identify learning gaps, or demonstrate value to parents and students.

How AI Powers Every Stage

Unlike tools that bolt AI on as an afterthought, Langopia's AI is deeply integrated into every layer of the platform — from class preparation to post-class analysis and student practice.

📝

AI Lesson Planning Assistant

Teachers describe what they want to teach (or upload a PDF, video, or article) and the AI generates a complete lesson plan — including timing, objectives, warm-up activities, and materials. Chat with the AI to refine the plan until it's perfect.

🎯

Automatic Exercise Generation

From any content source — a YouTube video, a news article, a podcast — Langopia's AI generates CEFR-aligned exercises across 11+ types: tap-to-order, fill-in-the-blank, listen & repeat, complete the chat, watch & reflect, multiple choice, and more. Each exercise adapts to the student's level.

🎙️

Real-Time Class Transcription

Every live class is transcribed in real-time using Whisper with speaker diarization. The AI identifies who said what, tracks speaking time per student, and captures every language interaction for later analysis.

🧠

Post-Class AI Analysis (GPT-4o)

After each class, GPT-4o analyzes the full transcript to extract: vocabulary used (with CEFR tags), grammar patterns and errors, pronunciation observations, participation metrics, and personalized improvement suggestions for each student.

📊

Automated Student Reports

Instead of teachers spending 20+ minutes writing reports after each class, Langopia generates detailed, professional reports automatically — including vocabulary highlights, grammar corrections, next steps, and CEFR progress indicators. Teachers just review and send.

🗣️

AI Voice & Pronunciation (XTTS)

Langopia includes its own text-to-speech engine (XTTS) for listen & repeat exercises with natural-sounding voices in multiple languages. Students can practice pronunciation and receive instant feedback — no teacher required.

🔄

Adaptive Learning Loop

The AI creates a continuous feedback loop: Class analysis → identifies gaps → generates targeted exercises → student practices → tracks improvement → informs next class plan. Every interaction makes the system smarter about each student's needs.
